A vintage travel poster for **LUMIVALE — The Valley Where the River Flows Upward**, a place that isn't there.

In Lumivale, the river does not run to the sea. It climbs. Silver water rises up the cliff face in a shining stair of falls, and small wooden boats sail upward through the twilight, past the village lights, toward the stars. Come at dusk, when the valley turns purple and the river turns to light — and sail up, against gravity, into the night sky. Nobody in Lumivale has ever seen the river reach the sea, because in Lumivale, the river goes the other way.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` (1024×1024) to the output directory, validating size and dimensions first.
